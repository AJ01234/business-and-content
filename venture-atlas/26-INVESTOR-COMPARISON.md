# 26 — Investor Comparison

## Executive thesis

The investors in this Atlas are not interchangeable. They optimize for different moments in company formation.

| Investor | Core selection lens | Earliest edge | Distinctive support | Best-fit founder signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| YC | Founder + problem + speed + scale | Very early | Batch/network + intense iteration | Builder with a credible venture-scale insight |
| Sequoia | Exceptional founder + unique insight + transformational market | Seed through growth | Long-term partnership, recruiting, customers, financing | Outlier founder with a large-growth market thesis |
| a16z | Technology + market + platform leverage | Seed through growth | Large operating platform | Builder in technology categories where platform leverage matters |
| HF0 | Repeat-founder execution + extreme focus | Seed/very early | Residency, environment, peer density | Prolific technical repeat founder |
| Entrepreneurs First | Individual talent before team/idea | Pre-team/pre-idea | Cofounder formation + company building | Exceptional individual with unusual edge |
| NFX | Founder + market + network effects | Pre-seed/seed | Network-effects playbooks + Guild | Founder whose product can compound through networks |
| General Catalyst | Founder + hard problem + transformation | Early through growth | Global thematic expertise + operating platform | Mission-aligned builder solving consequential problems |

## The important difference

**YC asks:** can this team become a very large company quickly?  
**Sequoia asks:** is this an exceptional founder with a unique insight into a market that can become transformational?  
**a16z asks:** is there technology/platform leverage and can the firm materially accelerate it?  
**HF0 asks:** can an already-proven builder compress years of work into months?  
**EF asks:** is this individual unusually capable before the company exists?  
**NFX asks:** where is the compounding network effect?  
**GC asks:** why this founder, on this hard problem, now—and can the result endure?

## Source notes

Sequoia explicitly describes seeking exceptional founders with unique insight in markets poised for large growth, while preferring early involvement and long-term partnership. https://sequoiacap.com/article/sequoia-and-seed-investing

a16z describes a platform model built around operators and invests from seed through growth across AI, bio/health, consumer, crypto, enterprise, fintech, games, infrastructure and American dynamism. https://a16z.com/about/

HF0 publicly describes itself as the residency for repeat founders. https://www.hf0.com/

NFX says it focuses on founding team and market opportunity and typically invests pre-seed and seed, with network effects central to its thesis. https://www.nfx.com/about

General Catalyst describes early company building, global thematic expertise and the question “Why them on this problem?” as central to its approach. https://www.generalcatalyst.com/stories/focusing-on-europe-a-conversation-with-gcs-london-based-investment-team

EF's defining model is talent investment: finding exceptional individuals before they necessarily have a team or company. https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/tech-and-ai/our-insights/the-people-first-approach-to-business-building
