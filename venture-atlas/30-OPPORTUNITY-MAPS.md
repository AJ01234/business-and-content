# 30 — Opportunity Maps

## Opportunity model

A venture opportunity is strongest when five forces overlap:

```text
Large pain
   ×
New capability
   ×
Favorable timing
   ×
Defensible distribution/data
   ×
Venture-scale expansion
```

## Opportunity A — AI employees for regulated operations

**Problem:** regulated industries contain repetitive, document-heavy workflows where human review is expensive.

**Why now:** capable multimodal models + agent tooling + better integration infrastructure.

**Wedge:** one workflow with clear ROI and human-auditable output.

**Expansion:** adjacent workflows → system of record → workflow network/data moat.

**Risks:** liability, compliance, model reliability.

## Opportunity B — Industrial AI without replacing hardware

**Problem:** factories and facilities already have cameras, sensors and software but lack intelligence layers.

**Wedge:** software that turns existing infrastructure into an autonomous monitoring/action system.

**Expansion:** more workflows → more sites → proprietary operational data.

**Risks:** integration, false positives, long sales cycles.

## Opportunity C — AI-native compliance infrastructure

**Problem:** regulation creates recurring evidence, reporting and monitoring work.

**Wedge:** one regulated workflow with verifiable source citations and audit trails.

**Expansion:** adjacent compliance processes + system-of-record position.

**Risks:** regulation changes and trust requirements.

## Opportunity D — Agent evaluation and reliability

**Problem:** businesses can deploy AI agents faster than they can measure whether agents work reliably.

**Wedge:** simulation/evaluation for one high-value agent category.

**Expansion:** evaluation → observability → deployment policy → data flywheel.

**Risks:** platform vendors may build native evaluation.

## Opportunity E — Physical-world data infrastructure

**Problem:** robotics needs high-quality real-world data, simulation and feedback loops.

**Wedge:** one physical environment with a measurable training/evaluation bottleneck.

**Expansion:** more environments + more task diversity + data network effects.

**Risks:** hardware fragmentation and capital requirements.

## Opportunity F — India-first vertical operating systems

**Problem:** Indian businesses often run on fragmented workflows, WhatsApp, spreadsheets and legacy software.

**Wedge:** one high-frequency business workflow with a measurable cash-flow outcome.

**Expansion:** payments, financing, procurement, payroll or marketplace layers.

**Risks:** low willingness to pay, fragmented buyers and operational intensity.
