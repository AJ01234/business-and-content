# 26 — YC 2026 Company Profiles

These are **illustrative research profiles**, chosen to expose different selection patterns. They are not a ranking.

## CellType — W2026

**What:** Agentic drug discovery using biological foundation models.  
**Signal:** Frontier technical capability + huge healthcare problem + validation with pharma.  
**Why it fits the Atlas:** The technology is not a cosmetic layer; it is the core mechanism for attacking a historically expensive discovery process.  
**Main risk:** Scientific validity, regulatory path and long development cycles.

## Beacon Health — W2026

**What:** AI employees for primary-care workflows such as screenings, prior authorizations and referrals.  
**Signal:** Repetitive high-cost workflow + clear buyer + measurable operational value.  
**Why it fits:** A strong example of “AI changes workflow economics” rather than “AI as a feature.”  
**Main risk:** Clinical trust, integration and workflow liability.

## Shiraz AI — S2026

**What:** Robots that learn new factory tasks from a human demonstration.  
**Signal:** Physical AI + manufacturing + data feedback.  
**Why it fits:** The physical environment creates a harder technical problem and potentially stronger proprietary data.  
**Main risk:** Deployment reliability and hardware economics.

## OpenVector — S2026

**What:** Turns existing cameras into AI-powered operational systems without requiring new hardware.  
**Signal:** Software wedge over installed infrastructure.  
**Why it fits:** The company attacks a distribution and deployment bottleneck: customers already own the sensors.  
**Main risk:** Accuracy, integrations and incumbent computer-vision vendors.

## Cerenovus — S2026

**What:** Aggregates company information into a knowledge graph for humans and AI agents and maps organizational workflows.  
**Signal:** Enterprise knowledge fragmentation + agent infrastructure.  
**Why it fits:** It attacks the context bottleneck that limits AI agents inside organizations.  
**Main risk:** Platform dependence and competition from suites with native enterprise knowledge layers.

## GodHands — F2026

**What:** A living knowledge base for engineering teams spanning code, docs and decisions.  
**Signal:** Engineering context + AI + system-of-record potential.  
**Why it fits:** Strong example of AI being embedded in a workflow rather than presented as a standalone assistant.  
**Main risk:** GitHub/Atlassian/cloud vendors can expand into adjacent territory.

## twentyfour26 — F2026

**What:** Autonomous CNC machining cells that turn CAD files into same-day custom metal parts.  
**Signal:** Industrial automation + speed + physical-world execution.  
**Why it fits:** The wedge connects software instructions to a real production bottleneck.  
**Main risk:** Manufacturing reliability, capex and quality assurance.

## Cross-profile pattern

The 2026 sample repeatedly combines:

**hard problem + technical leverage + narrow workflow wedge + large expansion surface.**

That pattern is stronger than simply counting AI labels.
