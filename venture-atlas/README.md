# Venture Atlas — Complete Research Edition

**Status:** Completed research edition v1.0  
**Date:** 22 August 2026

## Mission

Reverse-engineer how elite startup investors identify venture-scale companies, then convert those observations into testable founder and market frameworks.

The original Atlas methodology requires evidence over opinion, separation of fact from inference, consistent startup evaluation, first-principles problem analysis, and long-term thinking.

## Volumes

1. Executive Summary / Methodology — existing Atlas foundation
2. Y Combinator Atlas — Chapters 13–25
3. Investor Comparison — Sequoia, a16z, HF0, Entrepreneurs First, NFX, General Catalyst
4. Founder Patterns — cross-investor selection signals
5. Market Maps — AI, enterprise, fintech, healthcare, robotics/hard tech, marketplaces
6. Opportunity Maps — where technology + pain + market structure create venture opportunities
7. White Space — under-served categories, with warnings against treating low VC activity as proof of opportunity
8. Founder Playbook — how to convert the evidence into action
9. Final Synthesis — unified investor model and falsifiable hypotheses
10. References — primary sources and research notes

## Evidence standard

- **FACT:** directly supported by an official source or clearly documented dataset.
- **OBSERVATION:** repeated pattern in the evidence.
- **INFERENCE:** explanation derived from multiple observations.
- **HYPOTHESIS:** testable claim that could be disproved.

The Atlas deliberately does not claim to have discovered any investor's secret scoring algorithm.
